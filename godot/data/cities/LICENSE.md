# City circuit data

`*.json` here is derived from OpenStreetMap by `tools/cities/build_cities.py`: road network, circuit loop,
building footprints and heights, parks, water and mapped trees.

© OpenStreetMap contributors. The data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0),
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

- The game credits OSM under Settings → Credits. Every JSON file also carries an `attribution` field.
- These derived files are a Derivative Database and remain under ODbL. Screenshots and the game are Produced
  Works and need attribution only.
- Generated frontage buildings (the blocks the builder adds where OSM has no footprint) are GRIDLINE's own.
